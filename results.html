01 · Statistical mechanics · Bayesian

Bayesian inference on the 2D Ising model

Simulate a lattice of spins with Metropolis Monte Carlo, then infer the critical temperature and exponents with MCMC by finite-size scaling across four lattice sizes — benchmarked against the exact Onsager (1944) solution.

Tc = 2.268
critical temperature · finite-size scaling, L = 16–64 · 95% HDI [2.265, 2.271] · Onsager exact 2.2692
β = 0.113
critical exponent · 95% HDI [0.101, 0.126], exact 1/8; ν = 0.98 [0.92, 1.05], exact 1
Tc = 2.37
a power-law fit to a single 32×32 lattice misses Tc by 0.1 and cannot pin β — the reason for finite-size scaling

02 · Deep learning · Dynamical systems

Neural ODEs with a Hamiltonian prior

Learn the right-hand side of conservative ODEs from short noisy trajectories. A Hamiltonian Neural Network builds the symplectic structure into the architecture. A controlled ablation on Lotka-Volterra (5 seeds, with a coordinate control) shows when that helps: on orbits the models never saw, once the training data cover more than one orbit.

5.0 % → 2.4 %
relative error on five unseen Lotka-Volterra orbits, trained on three trajectories · MLP in log-coordinates → Hamiltonian NN, same parameter budget (5 seeds)
8.6 % → 2.2 %
drift of the conserved H on the same unseen orbits; with a single training trajectory no model generalises (17–18 % error)
8.3 ± 2.4 %
relative extrapolation error on the damped pendulum (baseline)

03 · Deep learning · MLOps

Reconstruction-based anomaly detection · CMAPSS

An LSTM autoencoder trained on healthy turbofan windows from NASA CMAPSS FD001; the reconstruction error becomes the anomaly score, with the threshold fixed as the 99th-percentile of the healthy validation error. Served as a typed FastAPI service, a Streamlit demo and a two-service Docker stack.

04 · ML for science · PDEs

Physics-informed neural networks

The PDE residual, computed by autodifferentiating the network's own output, becomes part of the loss — so the model is trained to satisfy the heat and Burgers equations directly, without a mesh and without solved-PDE training data.
